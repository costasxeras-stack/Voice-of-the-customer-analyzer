Why do shoppers say they would not recommend a product?

Real reviews from a women's clothing shop, where every reviewer also said whether they would recommend the item. A local AI model found the sentences that sound like a "would not recommend" review, and grouped them by complaint. Each theme is ranked by how much more often it appears in unhappy reviews than in happy ones.

What to fix first

Share of reviews with at least one complaint sentence in each theme. Pink is reviews that would not recommend the item, green is a random sample of the same number that would.
