# Product brief

## Problem

Product and merchandising teams use reviews to decide what to fix, but they cannot read thousands. Keyword dashboards count mentions without telling praise from complaint. Reading a sample depends on who reads what. Both can put the wrong problem at the top of the list.

## Users

- **Product managers and merchandisers** who decide what to change in a product, its size guide or its listing.
- **Customer insight analysts** who need a repeatable first cut of open-text feedback.
- **Hiring managers** reading this portfolio, who want to see how I turn unstructured feedback into a decision.

## Goals

1. Rank complaint themes by how strongly they go with an unhappy outcome, not by how often a word appears.
2. Show the evidence: real sentences for every theme.
3. Show where a simple keyword approach would mislead.
4. Run without paid AI calls, so it can be repeated on new reviews for free.

## Non-goals for 0.1.0

- Live data or a trend over time. The dataset has no dates.
- Product-level drill-down.
- Checked theme definitions. Theme names are labels for groups found by the model.

## Success measures

This is a portfolio piece, so there is no usage target. It succeeds if a PM reading the results page could say what to fix first and why, and check the evidence themselves.
